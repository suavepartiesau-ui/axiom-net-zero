Before you write in.

What happens after I initiate?

Your message opens directly in your own email client, addressed to the relevant department. Nothing is sent automatically or stored on a server we control.

How long until I hear back?

We're a small, decentralised team, so response times vary by department. Board and media inquiries are prioritised; general questions may take longer.

Is this a paid service?

Community-facing work is not. Sector Specific Consulting is a paid engagement — see the Sectors page for how those conversations start.

I want to start a node in my area.

Select General Inquiries and tell us what resource you're working with. Node autonomy means we don't gatekeep who can start one — we just need to know you exist.

Media & Press

Covering our work.

Journalists and researchers are welcome to select General Inquiries. We'd rather a claim about us be checked against the ledger than repeated from a press release.

Partnerships

Working alongside us.

Organisations with an existing resource — solar infrastructure, sensor networks, volunteer capacity — should reach out via the relevant department directly.
